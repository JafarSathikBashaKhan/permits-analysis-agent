## 3\. **Grace Period & Status Handling**

-   Two scenarios:
    
    -   **Without grace period:** Immediate move to Payment Failed
        
    -   **With grace period:**
        
        -   Waiting for Payment
            
        -   Due to be Closed
            
        -   Auto Cancel (after breach) [\[Permission...on with Jo | Meeting\]](https://teams.microsoft.com/l/meeting/details?eventId=AAMkADRhODcwMTc5LWU5MmItNDcwYy05MzIzLWE0MzNmZmM5YmViYgFRAAgI3saDNxAAAEYAAAAA8Yjw7HkDgkGw78Fblge0OgcAkdj41iWNBEKgL1IpqCBSmgAAAAABDQAAkdj41iWNBEKgL1IpqCBSmgABjJCOoQAAEA%3d%3d)
            
-   Reinstatement logic:
    
    -   Returns to previous status depending on configuration (Payment Failed / Due to be Closed) [\[Permission...on with Jo | Meeting\]](https://teams.microsoft.com/l/meeting/details?eventId=AAMkADRhODcwMTc5LWU5MmItNDcwYy05MzIzLWE0MzNmZmM5YmViYgFRAAgI3saDNxAAAEYAAAAA8Yjw7HkDgkGw78Fblge0OgcAkdj41iWNBEKgL1IpqCBSmgAAAAABDQAAkdj41iWNBEKgL1IpqCBSmgABjJCOoQAAEA%3d%3d)
