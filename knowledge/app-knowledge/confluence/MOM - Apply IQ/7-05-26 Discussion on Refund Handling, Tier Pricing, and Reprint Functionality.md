## **Decisions / Confirmations**

-   Refund failure handling with manual processing confirmed
    
-   Tier pricing logic for temporary permits confirmed
    
-   No restriction between buffer days and short notice fee
    
-   Reprint available without dependency on print partner
    
-   Lost permit fee must be configurable and optional
    
-   Reprint identification mandatory for fraud prevention
    
-   No limit required for reprint count
