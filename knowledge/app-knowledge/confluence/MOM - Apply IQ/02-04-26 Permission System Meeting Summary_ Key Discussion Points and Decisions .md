### **1.3 Bay Types & Mapping Behaviour**

-   Streets may have:
    
    -   Single bay type OR
        
    -   Multiple bay types (e.g., resident, shared use). [\[Permission...on with Jo | Meeting\]](https://teams.microsoft.com/l/meeting/details?eventId=AAMkADRhODcwMTc5LWU5MmItNDcwYy05MzIzLWE0MzNmZmM5YmViYgFRAAgI3pBKyI9AAEYAAAAA8Yjw7HkDgkGw78Fblge0OgcAkdj41iWNBEKgL1IpqCBSmgAAAAABDQAAkdj41iWNBEKgL1IpqCBSmgABjJCOoQAAEA%3d%3d)

    -   Bay types are driven by the **map API**, not back-office configuration. -   System should:
    
    -   Require closing (end date) of existing pricing before adding new pricing.     -   Time-bound pricing overrides default pricing during its active period. ## **2\. Decisions Taken**

-   Same bay type selection is valid for different physical locations (when distinguished via comments). [\[Permission...on with Jo | Meeting\]](https://teams.microsoft.com/l/meeting/details?eventId=AAMkADRhODcwMTc5LWU5MmItNDcwYy05MzIzLWE0MzNmZmM5YmViYgFRAAgI3pBKyI9AAEYAAAAA8Yjw7HkDgkGw78Fblge0OgcAkdj41iWNBEKgL1IpqCBSmgAAAAABDQAAkdj41iWNBEKgL1IpqCBSmgABjJCOoQAAEA%3d%3d)
    
-   Bay types will be **dictated by map integration**, not system configuration. [\[Permission...on with Jo | Meeting\]](https://teams.microsoft.com/l/meeting/details?eventId=AAMkADRhODcwMTc5LWU5MmItNDcwYy05MzIzLWE0MzNmZmM5YmViYgFRAAgI3pBKyI9AAEYAAAAA8Yjw7HkDgkGw78Fblge0OgcAkdj41iWNBEKgL1IpqCBSmgAAAAABDQAAkdj41iWNBEKgL1IpqCBSmgABjJCOoQAAEA%3d%3d)
    
-   All mapped bay types must have pricing configured. [\[Permission...on with Jo | Meeting\]](https://teams.microsoft.com/l/meeting/details?eventId=AAMkADRhODcwMTc5LWU5MmItNDcwYy05MzIzLWE0MzNmZmM5YmViYgFRAAgI3pBKyI9AAEYAAAAA8Yjw7HkDgkGw78Fblge0OgcAkdj41iWNBEKgL1IpqCBSmgAAAAABDQAAkdj41iWNBEKgL1IpqCBSmgABjJCOoQAAEA%3d%3d)
    
-   Pricing rules require proper lifecycle (end date before new rule). [\[Permission...on with Jo | Meeting\]](https://teams.microsoft.com/l/meeting/details?eventId=AAMkADRhODcwMTc5LWU5MmItNDcwYy05MzIzLWE0MzNmZmM5YmViYgFRAAgI3pBKyI9AAEYAAAAA8Yjw7HkDgkGw78Fblge0OgcAkdj41iWNBEKgL1IpqCBSmgAAAAABDQAAkdj41iWNBEKgL1IpqCBSmgABjJCOoQAAEA%3d%3d)
    
-   Separate configuration required for suspension vs dispensation. [\[Permission...on with Jo | Meeting\]](https://teams.microsoft.com/l/meeting/details?eventId=AAMkADRhODcwMTc5LWU5MmItNDcwYy05MzIzLWE0MzNmZmM5YmViYgFRAAgI3pBKyI9AAEYAAAAA8Yjw7HkDgkGw78Fblge0OgcAkdj41iWNBEKgL1IpqCBSmgAAAAABDQAAkdj41iWNBEKgL1IpqCBSmgABjJCOoQAAEA%3d%3d)
    
-   Workflow settings to be handled at contract level.
