## **3\. Address Capture Requirement**

-   Current gap identified:
    
    -   No address captured during car park permit purchase. [\[Permission...on with Jo | Meeting\]](https://teams.microsoft.com/l/meeting/details?eventId=AAMkADRhODcwMTc5LWU5MmItNDcwYy05MzIzLWE0MzNmZmM5YmViYgFRAAgI3q_5aRVAAEYAAAAA8Yjw7HkDgkGw78Fblge0OgcAkdj41iWNBEKgL1IpqCBSmgAAAAABDQAAkdj41iWNBEKgL1IpqCBSmgABjJCOoQAAEA%3d%3d)
        
-   Decision:
    
    -   **Address is required**, especially for:
        
        -   Paper permits (postal delivery)
            
        -   White mail / correspondence
            
    -   Also potentially required for **payment integration (3DS2 billing address)**. [\[Permission...on with Jo | Meeting\]](https://teams.microsoft.com/l/meeting/details?eventId=AAMkADRhODcwMTc5LWU5MmItNDcwYy05MzIzLWE0MzNmZmM5YmViYgFRAAgI3q_5aRVAAEYAAAAA8Yjw7HkDgkGw78Fblge0OgcAkdj41iWNBEKgL1IpqCBSmgAAAAABDQAAkdj41iWNBEKgL1IpqCBSmgABjJCOoQAAEA%3d%3d)
        
-   Agreed approach:
    
    -   Introduce an **Address step/tab** in the journey.
        
    -   Maintain consistency with other applications (Address → Vehicle → Pricing → Checkout). [\[Permission...on with Jo | Meeting\]](https://teams.microsoft.com/l/meeting/details?eventId=AAMkADRhODcwMTc5LWU5MmItNDcwYy05MzIzLWE0MzNmZmM5YmViYgFRAAgI3q_5aRVAAEYAAAAA8Yjw7HkDgkGw78Fblge0OgcAkdj41iWNBEKgL1IpqCBSmgAAAAABDQAAkdj41iWNBEKgL1IpqCBSmgABjJCOoQAAEA%3d%3d)

## **5\. Availability & Permit Allocation Logic**

-   General rule:
    
    -   Purchasing a permit **does NOT guarantee parking space availability**. [\[Permission...on with Jo | Meeting\]](https://teams.microsoft.com/l/meeting/details?eventId=AAMkADRhODcwMTc5LWU5MmItNDcwYy05MzIzLWE0MzNmZmM5YmViYgFRAAgI3q_5aRVAAEYAAAAA8Yjw7HkDgkGw78Fblge0OgcAkdj41iWNBEKgL1IpqCBSmgAAAAABDQAAkdj41iWNBEKgL1IpqCBSmgABjJCOoQAAEA%3d%3d)
        
    -   Parking is based on **first-come, first-serve at time of use**. [\[Permission...on with Jo | Meeting\]](https://teams.microsoft.com/l/meeting/details?eventId=AAMkADRhODcwMTc5LWU5MmItNDcwYy05MzIzLWE0MzNmZmM5YmViYgFRAAgI3q_5aRVAAEYAAAAA8Yjw7HkDgkGw78Fblge0OgcAkdj41iWNBEKgL1IpqCBSmgAAAAABDQAAkdj41iWNBEKgL1IpqCBSmgABjJCOoQAAEA%3d%3d)
        
-   Availability tracking:
    
    -   Typically **not required** for most councils. [\[Permission...on with Jo | Meeting\]](https://teams.microsoft.com/l/meeting/details?eventId=AAMkADRhODcwMTc5LWU5MmItNDcwYy05MzIzLWE0MzNmZmM5YmViYgFRAAgI3q_5aRVAAEYAAAAA8Yjw7HkDgkGw78Fblge0OgcAkdj41iWNBEKgL1IpqCBSmgAAAAABDQAAkdj41iWNBEKgL1IpqCBSmgABjJCOoQAAEA%3d%3d)
        
    -   Exception:
        
        -   Non-zonal parking sessions / visitor vouchers
            
        -   Availability validated based on:
            
            -   Total spaces configured
                
            -   Number of active sessions per day [\[Permission...on with Jo | Meeting\]](https://teams.microsoft.com/l/meeting/details?eventId=AAMkADRhODcwMTc5LWU5MmItNDcwYy05MzIzLWE0MzNmZmM5YmViYgFRAAgI3q_5aRVAAEYAAAAA8Yjw7HkDgkGw78Fblge0OgcAkdj41iWNBEKgL1IpqCBSmgAAAAABDQAAkdj41iWNBEKgL1IpqCBSmgABjJCOoQAAEA%3d%3d)

## **12\. Key Decisions Summary**

-   Address capture is mandatory in application flow.
    
-   Filters must be dynamic (not static).
    
-   Permit purchase does not guarantee parking availability.
    
-   Waiting list and permit limits to be supported.
    
-   Application flow must be standardized across modules.
