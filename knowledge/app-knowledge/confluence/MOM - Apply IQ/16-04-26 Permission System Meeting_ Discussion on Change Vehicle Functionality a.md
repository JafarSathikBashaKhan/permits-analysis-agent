## **3\. Decisions Summary**

-   Change Vehicle scoped only to linked vehicles
    
-   Licensing requires **separate base templates (Market & Pavement)**
    
-   Builder-driven flexibility is essential (fields, mandatory rules, discounts)
    
-   Licensing:
    
    -   Non-zonal
        
    -   No diesel surcharge
        
-   Vehicle section:
    
    -   Optional for licensing
