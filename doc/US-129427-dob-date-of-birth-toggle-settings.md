## Acceptance Criteria

**** 
**Given** the Date of Birth toggle is available in Contract Settings,
**When** the toggle is **disabled**,
**Then** the Date of Birth field should remain **optional** in both the Customer Portal and BO during applicant account creation.
 

 
**Given** the Date of Birth toggle is available in Contract Settings,
**When** the toggle is **enabled**,
**Then** the Date of Birth field should be **mandatory** in both the Customer Portal and BO.
