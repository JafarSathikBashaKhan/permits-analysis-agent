## Acceptance Criteria

Given a Super Admin, Contract Admin, BO User, or BO Manager
 
 
When they are in the Back Office (BO) applicant creation screen
 
Then they should have the option 'Paper Reminder' 

 
**Note: **This option is not available in the Customer Portal applicant creation screen. 

 
When creating an applicant account with the 'Paper Reminder' option 
 
Then the applicant should receive a white mail reminder letter when their application is up for renewal 

 
**Note**: This is specifically for applicants who do not have an email. The applicant account is created by the BO User using a generic email, and all communications like approval or password setup are handled by the Back Office User. Only the white mail reminder is sent directly to the applicant.
