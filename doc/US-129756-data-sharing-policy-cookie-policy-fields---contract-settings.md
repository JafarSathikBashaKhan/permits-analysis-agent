## Acceptance Criteria

**Given** a super admin or contract admin
 
**When** on the **Contract Settings** screen
**Then** they should see two new fields: 

- **Data Sharing Policy** 
 
- **Cookie Policy** 
 
 
And Both field should be optional 
And each field should allow configuring a URL 
And the configured URLs should be visible in the Customer Portal
